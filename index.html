What Is Bitway?

Its infrastructure is focused on connecting decentralized liquidity with financial strategies while combining onchain transparency with offchain execution.

The broader ecosystem includes yield products, Bitcoin infrastructure, lending, payments, token utilities, and blockchain-based financial applications.

Bitway Earn

Bitway Earn is a yield-generating vault product that allows users to deposit supported stablecoins and access market-neutral yield strategies.

The documented staking flow uses assets such as USDT or USDC and issues a vault token representing a user's share of the staked assets and associated yield.

Deposited assets can be deployed into market-neutral strategies, with generated returns periodically settled back into the staking vault.

Staking

Users deposit supported assets into the staking vault and receive a corresponding yield-bearing vault token.

Market-Neutral Strategies

Strategies are designed to target returns without relying on a single directional market movement.

Vault Tokens

Vault tokens represent a user's share of underlying deposited assets and accrued yield.

Bitway Earn Withdrawal Structure

Bitway Earn documents two withdrawal methods for its staking vaults: Normal Unstake and Flash Unstake.

  • Normal Unstake uses a waiting period before assets can be claimed.
  • Flash Unstake is designed for immediate withdrawal and applies a penalty according to product rules.
  • The withdrawal amount is calculated using the applicable vault-token exchange rate.

Bitcoin Infrastructure

Bitcoin is a central part of Bitway's broader infrastructure. The ecosystem includes a purpose-built Bitcoin-compatible Layer 1, Bitcoin bridge infrastructure, Bitcoin lending, and payment functionality.

The goal is to connect Bitcoin-based liquidity with programmable onchain financial applications and broader market opportunities.

Bitcoin Ledger

Bitway describes Bitcoin Ledger as a purpose-built Layer 1 blockchain designed for the Bitcoin-aligned onchain economy.

The network uses Proof-of-Stake infrastructure and supports blockchain operations, ecosystem applications, and the utility of the BTW token.

BTW also functions as the native gas asset for transactions and operations on Bitway Ledger.

Bitcoin Bridge

Bitway includes a Bitcoin bridge based on FROST technology and operated through Bitway validators.

The bridge is designed to connect Bitcoin with the broader Bitway ecosystem and support movement of Bitcoin-related value across the network infrastructure.

Bitcoin Lending

Bitway's ecosystem includes native Bitcoin lending infrastructure designed around non-custodial and permissionless lending concepts.

This component is intended to extend Bitcoin utility into onchain financial applications.

BTW Token

BTW is described as the native utility and governance token of the Bitway ecosystem.

The token supports network security, governance, economic incentives, staking, delegation, and ecosystem participation across Bitway's products and infrastructure.

Bitway Points and Referral System

Bitway Earn includes a documented BW Points and referral system associated with staking participation.

  • Users can earn BW Points based on qualifying deposits and staking duration.
  • Referral activity can provide additional BW Points under the applicable program rules.
  • Documentation states that BW Points may later be converted into BTW at a ratio to be announced.
